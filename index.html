Apple cider vinegar and the acetic acid it contains are key to the suggested mechanism of action of AlkaTide. Pectin can be used to supplement the recipe with soluble fibre. In practical terms, it depends on how much you eat, although soluble fibre can absorb water and help you feel full. AlkaTide's advantages

The formula may help with hunger and fullness. Apple cider vinegar's short-term effects on hunger and satiety have been studied. Pectin is an example of a soluble fibre that can aid in feeling full. However, studies have not found that all users consistently suppress their hunger, and the effects of appetite suppression seen in controlled trials could not last over time.

Support for managing weight is another frequently cited benefit. Due to differences in study design, participant characteristics, dose, and duration, there is still little evidence to support the slight increases in body weight or associated metrics seen in some clinical studies on apple cider vinegar. These findings shouldn't be interpreted as proof that AlkaTide alone results in significant or consistent weight loss.

Additionally, the components might contribute to intestinal health. When consumed in adequate amounts, the soluble fibre pectin may help maintain regular bowel movements. The drawback is that compared to foods like apples, citrus fruits, legumes, cereals, or certain fibre products, gummies may have substantially less fibre.

Overall, rather than causing a significant shift in metabolism, appetite, or body composition on its own, the most plausible hypothesis is that AlkaTide may offer some people minor additional help.

It is not appropriate to think of apple cider vinegar as a direct treatment for fat burning.
